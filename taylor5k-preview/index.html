Two events. Clear details.

Track Invitational · June 2027 target

The Kent Taylor Invitational Track Meet is being planned for early June 2027 at Coach Taylor Track at Norton High School. The exact date, format, eligibility, schedule, fees, and entry process are not yet confirmed. Confirmed information will appear here before entries open.

Community 5K · Fall 2027

The inaugural Kent Taylor 5K is planned for Fall 2027, centered at Norton High School. The exact date and start time, final course, and registration details are to be announced. A finish on the track named in Kent’s honor is the preferred plan.
